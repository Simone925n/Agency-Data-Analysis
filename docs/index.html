READ THE METRICS CORRECTLY

Definitions & guardrails

Contribution, not net profit
Uses the supplied Contribution_Margin_DKK field. Its cost basis is undocumented. Overhead, tax and cash collection are unavailable.
Weighted contribution margin
Sum of contribution ÷ sum of revenue × 100. Never an average of individual project margins.
Hour-budget variance
(Actual hours ÷ budgeted hours − 1) × 100. Portfolio variance uses the summed hours. Any positive variance counts as over budget.
Excess hours
Sum of max(actual − budget, 0) per project. This is effort exposure, not an estimate of recoverable profit.
Satisfaction
Simple mean of non-missing ratings. Missing values remain missing; coverage and sample size accompany results.
Time and status
Start dates identify cohorts. No completion dates, deadlines, billing dates or historical snapshots exist; lateness and revenue growth cannot be measured.
